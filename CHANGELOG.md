# Sift It Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- One search bar for every kind of file on your Mac: describe what you're looking for, or type the exact words.
- A preview made for each kind: the picture, the PDF page that matched, a video's frame at the moment, a recording's
  waveform with the stretch that matched, the passage or code with your words in bold.
- Opens each result where it matched: a recording or video at its moment, a PDF at its page, code at its line.
- Manage Folders: the folders to search, the model, the background indexer and its options.
- Indexing Status in the menu bar, with pause and resume.
